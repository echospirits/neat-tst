# Integrated CRM development notes — 0.2.3-dev

TST only. Existing production and historical 0.2 release artifacts remain unchanged.

## New and improved

- Pipeline now supports configurable detailed stages, account plans, estimates and territory/team filters. Log Visit records calls, email, texts, video meetings and private notes alongside the established physical-visit workflow. Duplicate Deals/Activities navigation is removed; old links redirect.
- Account Contacts gains person relationships, duplicate merging, aliases and reviewed business SMS with consent controls. History gains filters, completed work/events and explained relationship briefings.
- Organization Setup adds health thresholds, account categories/custom fields, teams/territories, visit guides and playbooks. Playbooks create ordinary Worklist tasks with replay and cancellation safeguards.
- My Schedule gains event/staff operations and reviewed route ordering. Log Visit adds guide snapshots and optional local draft recovery. Existing voice/photo/account context is preserved.
- Recipes gains editing, costing, account recommendations, approved branded public sheets and print support. Edits revoke shared materials.
- Analytics adds purchasing continuity, staff execution and territory forecast exports, preserving coverage/unknown-data distinctions. Optional reviewed AI cites authorized sources and never sends messages or creates tasks automatically.
- Mail adapters gain cursor-expiry reconciliation, recurring occurrence handling, backoff, retention cleanup, aliases/internal exclusions and original-message access. Twilio, Eventbrite, Routes and AI include application adapters and mocks/fallbacks.
- Compatible dependency patches reduce the audit to six package findings, with no critical findings. Remaining issues are documented rather than hidden.

## Activation and limits

Live providers require separate credentials, tenant consent/registration and acceptance; fixtures do not establish external delivery. Message bodies and personal phone capture are not ingested. Local drafts are not full offline sync. Daily purchase reports cannot prove individual order counts, revenue forecast accuracy or causation. Production promotion is separate.
