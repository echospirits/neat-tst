## Configuration and activation

No live provider is required for ordinary CRM release. Retain exact resource environment/host labels, app origin, encryption and side-effect controls. New source variable names are inventoried in release-lock.json; rehearsal variables are not hosted production requirements.

- Keep CRM_MAIL_ENABLED, CRM_SMS_ENABLED, CRM_ROUTES_ENABLED and CRM_AI_ENABLED unset/false until separate activation acceptance. SMS_ENVIRONMENT must match. Do not add live keys/scopes while packaging.

- Eventbrite organizer access, mail/SMS, Google Routes and reviewed AI require the [integration setup](../engineering/integration-setup.md) gates. Mocks do not establish live authorization/delivery/quality.
- Existing feature entitlements remain enforced. COMMUNICATIONS is a disabled-by-default Pilot depending on CORE_CRM; states are independent and teams/territories grant no privilege.

## Recovery

Disable affected optional integrations/jobs first. Preserve private correspondence/audits, consent and STOP history, approved shares and account data. Prefer a privacy-aware forward repair; do not drop tables/enums or automatically restore a snapshot that discards later business activity. If old import code is restored, pause wholesale imports until address protection is honored.
