# Neat 0.2.3 - What's new

Neat 0.2.3 brings better account follow-up, more useful field tools, and an easier way to get help.

- **Support inside Neat:** Report a question or issue, optionally include a screenshot, and follow replies and estimated fix dates in one place.
- **Clearer account navigation:** Retail and Restaurants labels make account types easier to recognize. External websites and maps open separately so your place in Neat stays open.
- **Better purchase browsing:** Browse all recent restaurant purchases, ordered by bottle volume, with easier paging through long product lists.
- **More useful opportunity ratings:** Eligible organizations get clear 0-5 commercial opportunity stars, with an explicit unrated state and supporting evidence.
- **Stronger account relationships:** Keep contact roles, relationships, account details, documents, and account history together. Pipeline plans and stages support your team's sales process.
- **Follow-up and field tools:** Worklist playbooks, guided visits, local visit drafts, event staffing, and route planning help teams prepare and follow through.
- **Sales materials:** Build recipe costings, review and approve recipe sheets, and share approved materials.
- **More dependable day-to-day work:** Improved account permissions, private information handling, password recovery, and account import safeguards.

Availability follows your organization's enabled features and configuration. Core CRM and Advanced Intelligence remain separate. State access is configured for each organization.

Connected email, business texting, and automatic contact suggestions remain a limited Echo Spirits Pilot. Live mail/texting, external event connections, optimized road routes, and AI assistance require separate setup and approval. Local visit drafts are not full offline synchronization.

Questions or feedback? Use Support in Neat.
