# Neat 0.10.2 — Scheduled-job usage controls

Status: **hotfix candidate; not released**. Prepared selectively from production `1076b724` for the owner's November 17, 2026 request to reduce Vercel usage. Exact-candidate checks, production promotion and hosted acceptance remain pending in the [checklist](0.10.2-checklist.md) and [validation](0.10.2-validation.md).

## Improved

- Production Supplies checks at most four times per 24 hours, waiting six hours after each automatic batch. Recovery watchdogs also run four times daily.
- Incomplete imports and failed attempts retain their checkpoints and resume at the next six-hour check. Historical backfill and error recovery therefore progress more slowly.
- Existing mailbox settings enforce a six-hour minimum while preserving saved team/access grants and any longer interval. Interrupted batches reserve their next slot before work; recovery and deployment handoff cannot restore frequent polling.
