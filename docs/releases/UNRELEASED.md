- **User-visible:** administrators may select any of twelve workflow values for a tenant tasting, and assigned tasters may do the same for their own assignments, at any time and in either direction. Calendar, Workflow / Upcoming, History and detail permit reopening Cancelled, denied or Completed labels. Draft retains the assignment. Taster Needs Edit/Cancelled require reasons; denial reasons remain optional. This supersedes the restrictive dropdown policy in the earlier November 6 entry below; its validation receipts remain historical.
- **Evidence and authorization:** explicit `workflowStatusOverride` is separate from actual taster/Agency/State milestones, import provenance and Visit/photo completion. Selecting later stages creates no earlier approvals; selecting Completed creates no Visit or completion timestamp and shows **Visit not logged** without proof. Real completion evidence remains immutable even when its label changes. Canonical Visit completion clears the override with history; material assignment/date/time/Agency changes reset manual status for the revised proposal with history. If retained cancellation or reassignment-only denial evidence would make a reopened proposal terminal again, the reset uses Needs Taster Response instead of clearing the override. Import Needs Review remains factual and is not a selectable workflow status.
