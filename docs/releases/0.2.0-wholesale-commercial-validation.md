## Implementation

- `WHOLESALE_COMMERCIAL_V1` / `COMMERCIAL_2026_10_V1` replaces the active model, not a score-to-stars conversion. Sales use category/style identity, comparable price basis, 750 ml equivalent quantities, absolute product depth and distinct-date persistence. Contacts, visits, assignments, relationship outcomes, strategic preferences and local-incumbent displacement do not grade the account.
- The source foundation is the longest contiguous certified daily block within the recent 90-day window, with a newer endpoint breaking ties. Latest and previous 30-day coverage are separate context. No missing-90-day cap, inferred zero day, cumulative-report summation or annual extrapolation is introduced.
- Saved current exact-location research is an alternative only when sales are unavailable. It groups scale and price/category fit, supports every tier, and keeps missing essential attributes distinct from negative findings. Venue-group seats, duplicated review platforms, missing optional amenities and historical claims cannot manufacture scale or fit.
- Shared accessible stars replace wholesale ratings in directory, detail, linked agency wholesale summaries, search, opportunity inbox/dashboard, research/targeting queues and reporting. Queries order directly by nullable whole rating, independent of evidence mode. JSON/CSV exports preserve zero/null and exact represented-period provenance.
- Discovery and explicit account-level acceptance require 3–5 stars and current eligibility. The separate legacy reactivation process requires at least one star before creating new automated work. Recalculation creates no pursuit or task. Explicitly accepted account follow-up has no automatic SKU/cocktail/competitor target.
- Daily successful sales-import orchestration performs the all-enabled-tenant sweep before retention. Research stays separate. Same-date revisions, corrected and zero-sales reports, backfills, tenant locks, source-error preservation and source-fingerprint checks are covered by tests.

## Recalculation and rollback

The score-only command `node --import tsx scripts/recalculate-opportunity-scores.ts --dry-run` is read-only by default. `--apply` persists all enabled active tenant accounts using saved inputs; `--organization` limits a tenant. `--include-inputs` is explicit because previews can contain private saved research. The development command refuses production. No import, crawler, research provider or LLM is called.

Before and during recalculation, database fingerprints confirmed unchanged research (393 rows), pursuits (21,582), tasks (9) and assessment pursuit preferences (46,158). Final coverage is verified from current assessment rows and per-run snapshots, not merely evaluator return values.

Rollback keeps this additive schema and historical snapshots. Prefer a forward fix plus score-only refresh. Stop the wholesale writer before reverting application code; an old writer does not produce valid current commercial stars and must not be allowed to overwrite the new current assessment state unnoticed. Do not delete rating history or apply a destructive down-migration. Deployment rollback alone does not reverse assessment data. Production promotion and its migration remain separately authorized release work.
