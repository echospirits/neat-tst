# Targeting and salesperson control

Commercial stars answer portfolio-compatible commercial strength. They do not measure buyer access or predict whether a rep will win an order. Contact availability, relationship, assignment, visits, tasks, buyer authority, strategic product role, targeting, dismissal, snooze and do-not-pursue preferences never modify the stars.

## Discovery and presentation

The default opportunity inbox and dashboard show eligible, unsnoozed, undismissed accounts with **3–5 commercial stars**, a current successful assessment, and no pending material-input refresh. This threshold is an initial implementation policy for attention, separate from the versioned scoring thresholds. Search without an explicit rating filter includes all ratings. All ratings, Zero stars and Unrated are explicit filters; individual integer filters are supported by the query contract. Stars sort across evidence modes, with null last for both ascending and descending order. A research-based five-star account therefore ranks above a sales-backed one-star account.

One shared read-only `CommercialOpportunityStars` component displays five positions, whole stars only, with the accessible label “Commercial opportunity: N of 5 stars.” Zero and Unrated have separate visible text. Pending, stale, error and source-error states remain separate; a failed refresh can display the last valid rating without presenting it as a current successful refresh. Closed/ineligible accounts remain separately labeled and excluded from actionable discovery. An account with no new-model assessment is Unrated; legacy priority is never converted to stars.

Converted surfaces: opportunity inbox; wholesale directory on mobile and desktop; account detail; wholesale accounts linked from agency detail; global account search; dashboard commercial counts; research queue ordering and research-depth selection; refresh monitoring. Retail-agency scoring and presentation remain unchanged. The directory retains normal alphabetical browsing by default; its Commercial opportunity sort uses nullable stars, never legacy priority or evidence mode. Existing Worklist/pursuit views retain their chosen actions and do not display historical numeric scores as current ratings. Weekly digests currently summarize actual work and sales and expose no wholesale numerical rating; no speculative commercial work is added to a digest.

Details disclose represented purchasing duration, compatible 750 ml equivalents, 30-day normalization, product depth and recurring core, price basis, limitations, model/calibration version, effective sales date, last calculation and independent last research dates. Public review ratings retain their separate “Public rating” label. No prominent secondary confidence score is introduced.

## Explicit acceptance and historical work

Assessment recalculation cannot create, reopen or assign a pursuit or task. A person may explicitly **Accept and create follow-up** for a current, eligible assessment at three stars or higher. Acceptance creates an account-level `COMMERCIAL_FOLLOW_UP` pursuit and one Worklist item; it does not select or recommend a product, cocktail, competitor replacement or best SKU. Its immutable snapshot contains the rating, account-level reasons and model version.

The acceptance action authenticates the user, scopes assessment/account preferences and assignee to the organization, rechecks eligibility, snooze, dismissal and pending/error status, and runs in a serializable transaction. An existing accepted/snoozed/open pursuit with work prevents duplication. A retry returns a review-existing-pursuit error rather than creating more work; the existing unique active-account key also protects concurrent acceptance. After commit, canonical calendar synchronization is scheduled for the new Worklist item. Scores never run this action.

Existing accepted pursuits preserve original targets, notes, tasks, detection evidence and outcomes. The separate legacy import-triggered reactivation workflow may create new work only at rating 1 or higher with READY status and no pending/suppressed/snoozed/dismissed state; it never creates new reactivation work for zero or unrated accounts. This is separate from assessment calculation and from the 3-star discovery/explicit-acceptance threshold. Existing service task updates and ordinary manual tasks remain available even at zero stars; the model does not manufacture nurture, tasting or nearby-mention work. Targeting is a deliberate user preference and may trigger the existing independent research workflow; it does not improve commercial stars.

Dismissal is account-level within this tenant, stored with a stable commercial account key. Legacy nonempty dismissal keys are honored until explicitly cleared. Daily recalculation preserves that preference and snooze dates while continuing to update commercial evidence. Dismissal and snooze only change visibility; clearing them never changes the grade.

## API and export

Authenticated `GET /api/wholesale/assessments` is tenant scoped and requires Wholesale Opportunities entitlement. It returns `commercial_rating` as integer 0–5 or null, plus `assessment_status`, `assessment_reason`, evidence mode, model version, `effective_assessment_date`, actual `sales_period_from`/`sales_period_through`/`represented_days`/`latest_source_date` (null for research-only), independent calculation/research dates and pending status. JSON preserves null; `format=csv` emits blank for null and literal zero for zero. CSV formula escaping is enabled. Pages contain at most 500 rows, with JSON `nextPage` or CSV `X-Next-Page`; exports default to all ratings, with `rating=zero`, `unrated`, `all`, `high` or 1–5 supported. No tenant may request another organization's assessments.
