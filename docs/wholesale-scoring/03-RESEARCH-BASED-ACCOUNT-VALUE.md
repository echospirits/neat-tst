# Research-based account value

Research is the full alternative when reliable sales are unavailable, including non-Ohio accounts. It cannot override low authoritative purchasing. Research-only accounts can receive any tier from zero through five.

Read saved exact-location, current, sourced evidence only. Calculation makes no research-provider calls, changes no research timestamps, and invents no precise bottle forecast.

## Essential evidence and fit tier

A confirmed current no-spirits/no-alcohol or beer-and-wine-only finding can establish zero. Missing information is not a confirmed negative.

Otherwise require an active spirits business, relevant menu product/pour resolved to catalog identity, a comparable bottle-price basis against the tenant’s included portfolio, and location-specific scale evidence. Use the same category/style and 0.60–1.25 price-ratio rules as sales. Public menu identity supplies retail-catalog positioning, not invoice price; cocktail prices cannot become bottle costs.

| Fit tier | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | All documented relevant comparable products materially mismatch the portfolio price range |
| 2 | One compatible product, without evidence that its relevant category is substantial |
| 4 | At least two distinct compatible product identities |
| 5 | Compatible product plus sourced dedicated/extensive/signature/specialized/central/core/featured/focused relevant category program |

Duplicate tenant SKUs and repeated source mentions add nothing. One incidental bottle cannot inherit an enormous venue’s whole scale. Conversely, one strongly supported core category can justify fit five without requiring many products.

## Likely scale matrix

Use the largest valid platform review count, never the sum, or explicitly documented individual-location seating.

| Base scale | At least one criterion |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Some valid reviews, seats or sourced recent activity |
| 2 | 75 reviews or 40 seats |
| 3 | 300 reviews or 100 seats |
| 4 | 1,000 reviews or 200 seats |
| 5 | 1,500 reviews or 300 seats, plus all gates below |

Four/five requires a documented spirits-led program: cocktail bar, extensive spirits/cocktail program, dedicated cocktail program or full cocktail menu. Otherwise scale is capped at three.

Five additionally requires current activity (recent reviews, weekly events or daily service/happy hour), plus service-occasion context (private dining/events, reservations, meals, patio/outdoor dining) or successful group affiliation. These are grouped gates, not stacked bonuses. Group affiliation only corroborates independently established location scale. When a capacity claim mentions a group, chain or multiple locations/venues, only an explicitly scoped individual figure such as “this location has 100 seats” counts; punctuation cannot detach a group total from its context.

Sentiment is secondary. A public rating below 3/5 on the most-reviewed platform constrains exceptional scale to four. Positive ratings add nothing by themselves.

Final stars = minimum(scale tier, fit tier). Missing essential scale, identity, source date or price fit yields Unrated. These are qualitative heuristics, not customer counts or volume forecasts.

## Examples and safeguards

- 1,600 location reviews, current activity, an extensive rum-led cocktail program, private dining and compatible exact menu identity: scale 5, fit 5, five stars.
- The same large venue with one incidental matching rum bottle: fit 2; at most two stars.
- Two platforms with 800 reviews each: use 800; scale three.
- A group with 1,000 seats across locations supplies no individual location seating.
- A $12 local vodka placement does not fit a $32 premium vodka portfolio or imply affinity for other categories.
- $16 cocktails and excellent reviews without resolved bottle positioning remain Unrated.
- Explicit former/historical programs or prior-year “in YYYY” examples do not establish current facts.
- Negated clauses such as “no patio” or “does not offer private dining” do not satisfy positive gates, but do not erase separate positive program evidence in the same saved fact. Missing optional amenities cannot lower a rating merely by being mentioned.

Some legacy saved research will remain Unrated because it lacks essential evidence for this rubric. That is an honest limitation. Recalculation must not launch research to manufacture a result.
