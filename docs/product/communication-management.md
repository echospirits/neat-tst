# Contact and interaction management

Communications is retired in `0.4.0` for Echo-only Neat. Desktop/mobile menus, mailbox connections, automatic email/appointment imports, business SMS, contact suggestions and the reviewed communication assistant have been removed, including direct endpoints and background workers.

Account Contacts still supports manual creation/import, editing, relationships, phone/email links and save-to-phone. Use Log Visit for physical visits or manually recorded phone/email/text/video/note interactions. Opening a phone/email application records initiation, not proof of completed communication or a physical visit.

Account timelines and briefings retain authorized historical evidence. Worklist remains the source of follow-ups, including the existing independent calendar synchronization. Existing communication history, contacts, consent/opt-out records and historical migrations remain stored; no database cleanup is part of retirement.
