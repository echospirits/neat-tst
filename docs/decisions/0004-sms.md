# ADR 0004 — Business SMS adapters

Status: accepted and implemented in integrated 0.2.3-dev.

Twilio is the initial provider behind SmsProvider; MockSmsProvider supports nonproduction acceptance without delivery. Each organization owns one configured connection and its sending identity. Credentials use existing encrypted token storage. Sending requires active membership, an enabled/registered identity, current consent, explicit reviewed input and environment activation. Inbound callbacks validate Twilio signatures and enforce bounded bodies.

Consent and opt-out are keyed by organization and normalized phone, with an append-only evidence ledger. Inbound STOP is applied even when the sender connection is disabled. Duplicate callbacks are harmless; uncertain sends remain UNKNOWN and are never automatically retried. Delivery transitions cannot regress a final delivery result. Shared phone matches require review when account identity is ambiguous.

Alternatives rejected: scraping personal phones, assumed consent, and automatic retries after ambiguous provider outcomes. This costs some manual reconciliation but prevents duplicate customer messages. Activation does not itself prove legal compliance or carrier approval; the tenant must complete its messaging registration and consent process.
