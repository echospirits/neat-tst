# Social Publishing

Mode: Operate. Extend Neat's established visual system under the owner-authorized Social Publishing pilot.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show the exact content, destination and editorial approval beside each independent delivery result. Keep public publication intentional and failure recovery actionable.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Neat's Chalkboard text, Copper actions, Patina focus, semantic surfaces and divider-led lists. Existing typography, surface radii and accessible field controls govern this extension. No generated artwork or new visual identity.

STORY: Administrators connect actual accounts and assign brand groups. Explicitly enrolled contributors draft and submit content for their assigned groups. Administrators approve an immutable revision for immediate or durable scheduled delivery, inspect destination results and resolve partial failures without re-sending successful destinations.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact page heading, explicit simulated/live/tenant-paused state, and three navigation areas lead to Posts & approval, Connections & groups, and Results & capabilities. Post rows show title, group, revision, review state, delivery state and Eastern timing. Connections separate provider setup/review from selected destination health and group mapping. The composer keeps group, shared starting caption, exact destinations, media and platform differences in one focused route.

FORM: Reuse PageHeader, ActionForm, SubmitButton, RecordPicker and LiveFilterForm. Lists and two-column forms wrap into one column on a 390 × 844 phone. Optional native finishing, preview and administrator diagnostics use disclosure. Working review/delivery states stay exposed. Uploaded media versions remain private and immutable; public URLs are minted near dispatch for one scoped file. Native finishing uses canonical Worklist tasks with downloadable media, copyable caption and recorded post-link completion.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Approve and schedule / Approve and publish now bind the exact destination IDs, copy, media order, options and timing. Edits withdraw pending approval. Simulated outcomes and manual completion remain visibly distinct from verified live provider evidence.

QUALITY BAR: No mobile horizontal page scrolling; 44px frequent controls; legible disabled/pending/error/success states; no credentials in client props; unauthorized tenant/group/admin routes rejected by server; native optional features never silently omitted. New Google destinations start with a separate empty venue-focused caption and an explicit policy acknowledgement.

## Evidence and boundaries

Browser screenshots use isolated synthetic fixtures; no real social account, Blob upload or public-post result is implied. No shipping raster assets were created. Shared DESIGN.md and its token sidecar remain unchanged by this extension.
