# Tastings

Mode: Operate. Extend the established Neat world under the user's detailed workflow and UI/UX brief.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make assigned tasting commitments and approval bottlenecks visible beside their next authorized action. Preserve the canonical Visit and account workflows.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Neat's Chalkboard text/sidebar, Copper actions, Patina focus, semantic surfaces, compact aligned lists and restrained rules. No competing visual identity or decorative assets.

STORY: Coordinators propose and track approvals. Assigned tasters review the precise assignment, respond and log a same-day photo-backed Visit. Sales stay source-qualified observations.

November 6 refinement: status dropdowns on the main calendar/workflow/history views and detail let administrators and assigned tasters choose any of twelve workflow values at any time, forward or backward, including reopening terminal labels. Draft retains the assignment. Required edit/cancellation reasons and optional denial reasons remain inline. The selected label stays distinct from recorded approvals, import-review facts and Visit execution: manual Completed without proof visibly says Visit not logged. Material revisions reset the manual status for the revised proposal with history; preserved cancellation/denial evidence must not silently close a reopened proposal again. Filters, scroll context, tenant/assignment boundaries and immutable Visit/photo evidence stay intact. No new visual-world, typography or token decision is introduced; existing DESIGN.md and the pre-existing sidecar are preserved.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact heading, workflow/calendar/history navigation and filters precede useful records. Detail begins with Agency, time, taster, execution and primary Visit action. Mobile uses a date agenda; desktop supports a seven-column month or week.

FORM: Existing Neat operational lists, monthly grid and shared field controls. User-pinned established world; no concept roll or generated comp is applicable. Signature interaction: assigned response and canonical Log Tasting Visit keep the Agency and tasting context.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
